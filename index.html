5. Neden

  • Yedek: çiftliğin, oyun silinse ya da cihaz değişse de geri gelmesi; çiftlik koduyla başka cihaza taşınması.
  • Kullanım verisi: oyunu iyileştirmek — hangi durakların oynandığını, nerede takılındığını, hangi hataların çıktığını ve çiftlik ekonomisinin dengesini görmek. Veriler reklam, pazarlama, profil çıkarma ya da izleme için kullanılmaz ve satılmaz.
  • Hız sınırı ve doğrulama: sunucuyu kötüye kullanıma karşı korumak.

Hukuki dayanak: yedek için hizmetin sunulması (KVKK md. 5/2-c; GDPR md. 6/1-b), kullanım verisi ve hizmet güvenliği için meşru menfaat (KVKK md. 5/2-f; GDPR md. 6/1-f). Kullanım verisi Ebeveyn alanından her zaman kapatılabilir.

9. Nasıl kapatılır ve silinir

Uygulamada ana menüde sağ üstteki Ebeveyn düğmesi → çarpma sorusu → Ebeveyn alanı:

  • Bulut yedeği — Kapalı: gönderim ve indirme hemen durur, bekleyen yedek atılır. Sunucudaki son kopya silinmez (silmek için aşağıya bakın). Oyun yalnız cihazda çalışmaya devam eder.
  • Kullanım verisi — Kapalı: olay kaydı hemen durur, cihazda bekleyen olaylar atılır ve sunucuya "kapalı" bilgisi gönderilir; sunucu bundan sonra bu çiftliğin olaylarını kaydetmez. Bir cihazda kapatılan seçim, aynı çiftliğe bağlı öteki cihazlarda da sonraki açılışta geçerli olur.
  • Çiftliği sunucudan sil (iki adımlı onay): sunucudaki çiftlik, bütün kayıt sürümleri, olaylar, çiftlik kodları ve sayaçlar silinir; bu çiftliğe bağlı öteki cihazların bağlantısı da kopar. Bu cihazda oyun kaydı kalır; yeni bir rastgele numara üretilir, yedek ve kullanım verisi kapalı duruma geçer. İnternet yoksa silme yapılmaz ve sonra denenmek üzere bekletilmez; ebeveyne yeniden denemesi söylenir. Aynı çiftliğe bağlı başka bir cihazda yedek açık kalırsa o cihaz kendi kaydını yeni bir çiftlik olarak yeniden yedekler; orada da kapatılmalıdır.
  • Uygulamayı silmek sunucudaki veriyi silmez; veriler yukarıdaki sürelerle silinir.
  • E-postayla talep: info@uca-apps.com. Veriler bir kişiye değil rastgele numaraya bağlı olduğundan, uygulama cihazdayken en güvenli yol Ebeveyn alanından silmektir.

1. Who we are

The developer of Blu: Farm Festival, responsible for the data in this policy:

  • Name: uca-apps
  • Contact: info@uca-apps.com

2. In short

  • The game plays fully offline. When online, progress is backed up to our server and in-game events (such as which stop was played or where a hint was asked for) are sent.
  • We do not collect name, email, phone, date of birth, location, advertising ID, device ID, device name, free text or screenshots. There is no sign-in. The child is never asked anything.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. The only outside library is the store's own payment library for the full game purchase (Google Play Billing; Apple StoreKit on iOS). Data goes only to our own server, hosted for us by Supabase (EU, Frankfurt).
  • The game is free to download; summer stops, the farm and free driving are always free. The other stops open with a one-time full game purchase (no subscription), placed behind the parent question. Payment is handled by Google Play (the App Store on iOS); no card or account details reach us.
  • In the Parent area, protected by a parent question, a parent can turn backup and usage data off separately and delete the farm from the server.

4. What we do not collect

Name, email, phone number, date of birth, address, location, advertising ID (AAID / IDFA), device ID (Android ID, IMEI etc.), device name or model, time zone, contacts, photos, audio, screenshots, free text, chat, browsing history. The app has no chat or communication between players.

5. Why

  • Backup: so the farm comes back if the game is deleted or the device changes, and can move to another device with a farm code.
  • Usage data: to improve the game — to see which stops are played, where children get stuck, which errors occur and whether the farm economy is balanced. It is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling or tracking and is not sold.
  • Rate limits and validation: to protect the server from abuse.

Legal basis: providing the service for backup (GDPR art. 6(1)(b); KVKK art. 5/2-c), legitimate interest for usage data and service security (GDPR art. 6(1)(f); KVKK art. 5/2-f). Usage data can always be turned off in the Parent area.

6. Where it is stored

In our database hosted by Supabase (Supabase Inc.) in the European Union — Frankfurt (Germany) region. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS). The app cannot reach the tables directly; it only calls a few server functions, and each farm reaches only its own data with its own key. Data is stored outside Turkey (EU, Germany), only for hosting and under the data processing agreement with the hosting provider.

8. Who we share it with

Only our hosting provider (Supabase, with its cloud infrastructure provider as sub-processor), only to store and process it for us. If the full game is bought, the payment and the purchase record are handled directly by the store (Google Play or the App Store) under its own terms and privacy policy; they are not sent to us. The device only keeps "full game unlocked"; if usage data is on, the store_purchase event in Annex A (without amount or payment details) tells our server that it was bought. Data is not sold and not shared with ad networks or data brokers. If the law requires it, data is disclosed only to the competent authorities and only as far as needed.

9. How to turn it off and delete it

In the app: the Parents button at the top right of the main menu → multiplication question → Parent area:

  • Cloud backup — Off: uploads and downloads stop at once; a waiting backup is dropped. The last copy on the server is not deleted (see below to delete it). The game keeps working on the device only.
  • Usage data — Off: event logging stops at once, events waiting on the device are dropped and "off" is sent to the server; the server then stops storing this farm's events. An off choice made on one device also applies on the other devices of the same farm from their next launch.
  • Delete farm from server (two-step confirmation): the farm, all save versions, events, farm codes and counters are deleted from the server; other devices joined to this farm lose their link too. The game on this device stays; a new random number is created and backup and usage data turn off. Without internet nothing is deleted and nothing is queued for later; the parent is told to try again. If another device of the same farm keeps backup on, it backs up its own save again as a new farm; turn it off there too.
  • Deleting the app does not delete server data; it is deleted after the periods above.
  • Requests by email: info@uca-apps.com. Because data is linked to a random number, not a person, deleting from the Parent area is the surest way while the app is on the device.

10. Children

Blu: Farm Festival is designed for children aged 4–8. Nothing is asked of the child; everything about network, accounts or data is in the parent area behind the parent question. The only link out of the app (this policy) is there too. The parent question keeps the child from changing these settings by accident.

11. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to processing and to complain (KVKK art. 11; in the EU GDPR art. 15–22 and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority). Because we link data to a random number, not a person, we can only find which farm a request is about while the app is on the device: delete it in the Parent area, or email the farm code shown there (valid for 15 minutes) to info@uca-apps.com. If the app has been deleted, the data cannot be matched to anyone and is deleted automatically after the periods above (at most 24 months).

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address. Effective date: 20 November 2026 (first version 19 November 2026).
